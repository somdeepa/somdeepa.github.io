Teaching

My teaching experience includes undergraduate courses in introductory and intermediate microeconomics. In my in-person instruction, I use a combination of worked examples, end-of-class Sheets, and classroom activities to help students actively apply economic concepts, strengthen their intuition, and connect economic models to real-world questions.

Sheets

In my in-person courses, I developed Sheets: brief paper exercises completed during the final few minutes of class. The Sheets ask students to reinforce the day's material by drawing graphs, solving short problems, and explaining their reasoning while the concepts are still fresh. As students work—often consulting their notes and discussing ideas with classmates—I move around the classroom to identify misconceptions, answer questions, and adjust future instruction where needed. Because the Sheets count toward participation, they also encourage collaboration and provide a low-stakes incentive for regular attendance.
